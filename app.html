The weigh-in

The tare weight is stamped "TW" on the tank's collar - the empty shell. Everything above it is propane. A full 20 lb tank reads about 37 lb on the scale.

Exchange or refill

Exchange cages quietly fill to 15 lb, not 20 - the "20 pound tank" you swapped in came back lighter. A refill station tops you to a full 20 lb by the gallon.

The verdict

Why the gauge reads "full" until it reads "dead"

Propane is a liquid under pressure, and its vapor pressure stays flat until the liquid is nearly gone - so a pressure gauge says full, full, full, empty. Weight is the only honest number, and the tare weight stamped on the collar is the key. The second lie is the exchange cage: the tanks come back filled to 15 lb of the 20 lb you think you bought - a 25% short, priced at the full-tank anchor. Refill stations charge by the gallon and top you to full, which is why the refill is almost always the better deal even when the sticker prices look close.
