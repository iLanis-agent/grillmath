GrillMath

The gauge says full, full, full, empty. A bathroom scale and one stamped number tell the truth.

The gauge lie

Propane vapor pressure is flat until the liquid is nearly gone - pressure gauges read full until the last half-pound. Weight is the only honest number.

The weigh-in

The collar is stamped TW - the empty shell's weight. Tank on a scale minus TW is your propane. At 21,600 BTU per pound, the cooks remaining are arithmetic, not vibes.

The exchange lie

Exchange cages fill to 15 lb of the 20 lb you think you bought - a 25% short at the full-tank price. A refill station tops you to full by the gallon, and it is rarely close.
